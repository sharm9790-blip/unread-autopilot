# vidIQ research pack (2026-11-15) - patterns to borrow, never stories to copy

Source: vidIQ outliers (YouTube Shorts, last 3 months, views vs the channel's normal) + Instagram/TikTok outlier
search (posts vs the creator's median) + rising YouTube searches. "x" = times the creator's usual views.

## 1. An animal warns that a family member is an impostor (strongest pattern right now)
- "If your dog ever starts barking at your mom..." - 650K, 9x (YouTube) and 840K (TikTok, "If your dog suddenly
  starts growling at your mom - do not tell it to stop")
- "If a black dog sleeps under your house in the Philippines..." - 38x (Instagram, Aswang folklore)
- "Can Dogs See Spirits?" - 218K
Use: pets (dog, cat, parrot, cow, goat) reacting to Mom/Dad/Grandma = proof they aren't who they seem.

## 2. A family member does the impossible (contradiction) - matches UNREAD's own ~1K winners
- "Every night at 3 a.m. my mom asks if I'm awake, but I can hear her snoring down the hall" - 5x on a 3K-sub channel
- "Your girlfriend is asleep right next to you... so who just texted you from outside your window?" - 174x (Instagram)
- "There's an Imposter Who Looks Exactly Like My Grandma!" - 682K
- **Rising search, +783% this month (~91K searches/month):** "if your mom ever says goodnight to you twice... lock
  your door" - people are literally searching this phrase. Write our own take on "Mom does X twice".

## 3. Grandma's rule / one warning
- "Never Be the Last One Awake at Grandma's House" - 10x on a 2K-sub channel
- "My grandmother had one warning... If a woman calls your name in the forest at night, don't answer" - 1.1M, 13x
- "Never Forget What's Under Grandma's Porch" - 993K

## 4. Regional legend opener ("In <place>, if ... at night") - fits UNREAD's top countries (India 26%, Philippines 16%)
- "In India, if someone knocks at night..." (Nale Ba, Bengaluru) - 1.3M, 11x
- "In Rajasthan, if a woman calls your name at night..." (Chudail) - 1.8M
- "In Japan, if a woman in a mask asks, 'Am I pretty?'..." - 984K
- A voice that copies a loved one to get you to open the door/window is the core of several 1M+ hits.
Use: open with the place + the rule, keep it a NEW story (don't retell the legend itself).

## 5. "N rules for the night shift at <place>" as a repeatable series
- One 4K-sub channel posts "Night Shift at <Truck Depot / Indian Mela / Train Yard / Highway Hotel> | 6 Horror Rules"
  every day: 6x-24x each. A fixed title template + a new place each day.
- "This Convenience Store Has Rules That Can Get You Killed!" - 301K, 8x
Use: a weekly UNREAD rules series in 25-30 s (3 rules, the 3rd broken = twist).

## 6. Formats that win on TikTok/Instagram (for reference)
- Interactive "#decide" / "What would you do?" series and numbered Parts ("Part 6") keep viewers coming back.
- Most hits are 60-180 s, but UNREAD's data says 25-30 s wins on our Shorts: keep ours short, borrow the hooks.

## 7. Philippines (UNREAD's #2 audience, 16%) - vidIQ 2026-11-15
- "If a black dog sleeps under your house in the Philippines..." 38x; "There's a city in the Philippines you're never
  supposed to visit... NEVER eat the food" (Biringan) 112x; "NEVER GO OUT AFTER 6 PM - the vampire of the province" 90x;
  "Why are these legs standing alone?" (manananggal) 1.3M.
Use: provincial house, the 6 PM curfew, a black dog under the house, food you must not eat; keep stories original.

## 8. Hindi/Hinglish horror (for a possible Hindi channel; not for UNREAD English titles)
- "सास ने कहा था, रात को किसी को नमक मत देना" (mother-in-law's rule: never give salt at night) - 2.5M, 23x
- "काली चौदस की रात... पीछे मत देखना!" (Kali Chaudas night... don't look back) - 5M, 240x
- "मंगलवार रात ग्यारह बजे खाली पूजा घर में घंटी किसने बजाई?" (who rang the bell in the empty prayer room) - 32x
- "दादी की तेरहवीं के बाद 12 चाबियाँ" (12 keys after Grandma's 13th-day rite) Part 3 - 1.1M
Same engine as UNREAD: a family elder's rule + a festival/ritual night + a numbered series. Hindi hits run 50-90 s.

## Title scores (vidIQ, 0-100, Shorts) for scheduled Nov 16-19 titles: 82-96, all strong.
Best: "My Cat Left a Baby Tooth on My Pillow. I Have No Kids." 96; "My Son Waves Goodnight to the Window. We're on the
Fifth Floor." 95; "Mom Packs Two Tiffins. I'm an Only Child." 93. Lowest: "Photo Album · Pt 1: Dad Cuts Someone Out of
Every Family Photo" 82 -> adding the contradiction ("...She's Always Next to Me.") scored 95.
Rule: titles = <impossible family fact>. <short contradiction that names who/where>. Two sentences beat one.
